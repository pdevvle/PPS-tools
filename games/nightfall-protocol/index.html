    Field log

      Field manual
      Move
      Click a tile. Blue range costs 1 action, amber range (dash) costs both. On touch, tap once to preview, again to move.
      Cover
      Shields show cover at the hovered tile. Half cover −20 to enemy aim, full cover −40. A unit with no cover facing the shooter is flanked: +50% crit.
      Shooting
      Firing ends the soldier's turn. Click a target, click again or press Enter to fire. Tab cycles targets.
      Overwatch
      When an enemy moves into sight, time slows and you see through your soldier's eyes. Aim and fire yourself: cover blocks the bullet, headshots crit, and low-Aim soldiers sway more. Wait too long and the shot is rolled automatically (−15 aim). Switch to fully automatic with the button at the top.
      Concealment
      Enemies don't know you're here. Red tiles are inside a patrol's detection range. Attacking breaks concealment.
      Explosives
      Grenades ignore cover, destroy it, and shred 1 armor.
      Keys
      1–8 actions · Tab next soldier/target · Enter confirm · Esc cancel · Q/E rotate camera · wheel zoom · Backspace end turn
