Nightfall · real-world sector prototype

Anthem and New River, Arizona

Two 600 m sectors built from real OpenStreetMap data and real elevation, in the game's toon low-poly style. Streets, buildings, washes, parking lots and every shop are where they really are. Desert plants, backyard block walls and abandoned cars are generated on top. Your squad of four generated survivors starts on a road. Click the ground to move them.

Loot sites by type

    Real versus generated

    • Real: terrain height, roads and footpaths, building footprints and types, washes and the New River, parking lots, pools, parks, mapped walls, and every shop, restaurant and service with its name.
    • Generated: building heights and roofs where the map has none, backyard block walls, saguaros, palo verdes, creosote and rocks, and parked or abandoned cars.
    • Loot types come from what each place is: groceries and restaurants give food, the dentist and vet give medicine, Ace Hardware and the car repair shop give tools.

    Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Elevation: Terrain Tiles on AWS (Mapzen terrarium).
