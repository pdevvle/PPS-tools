    Real groundA 200 m cut of the baked sector: heights, the 2 m movement grid, the house, barn, shed and outbuildings, roads, the wash and the plants. The buildable area is 140 m square.
    Built things change the gridWalls, gates, tanks, the kitchen and watch posts write wall or building and their cover into the grid when finished, as the bake does. Paths go round them, and gates let the ranch's own people through.
    Stand-in rulesWater drains at 0.5 L per person per hour, doubled from 10:00 to 18:00. Job choice is a simple score until people.md provides the real one, and stores stand in until settlement.md.
