Motion Lab

Everyone here runs Motion.update(fig, state, dt), the module the streaming and combat pages share. On the loop, four people climb a 15% ramp and go down six steps with a bolt-action rifle or a pistol, hands solved to the grips. On the course, two more vault a wall, climb a ladder, drop off a tower, turn on the spot, mantle a ledge and climb back down.

Feet stay where they landA foot bearing weight is pinned to the world, and hip, knee and ankle are solved to reach it. Walking swaps feet by the gait phase. Turning or changing stance steps a foot that has drifted too far.
Hands on the weaponEach hold places the weapon, from slung and low carry to port arms, ready and aim. The arms are solved to its grips. Firing the rifle works the bolt. Reloading loads a stripper clip into the rifle or changes the pistol's magazine and racks the slide.
Falls and get-upsA fall is a ragdoll of 19 particles with the shove of the hit, and the skeleton follows it. Getting up starts from wherever the body lies, either sitting up from the back or pushing up from the front.
