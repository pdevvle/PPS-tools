Character style · base figure

Base figure study

A plain starting point for the characters in a toon-shaded low-poly style. Every face is generated from a seed, and so is every body, so no two people look alike: press New people, or open the Lineup. Flat planes are lit in three hard tones. The head is sculpted with realistic anatomy (brow, eye sockets, cheekbones, nose, lips, chin) on a thick neck. Hair is left off: it will be a procedural layer, like clothing. The colours are solid, and the face is painted with only a few marks. Clothing, hair styles and gear would layer on top later.

What defines the look

  • Toon shading on flat planes. Each form has 8 to 12 sides, and light falls into three hard tones (lit, mid and shadow), so the planes of the jaw, nose, chest and limbs read like a painted poster.
  • A face sculpted with real anatomy at low-poly resolution: the eyes sit halfway up the head in sockets under a brow ridge, with cheekbones and hollows below them. The nose has a bridge, tip and nostril wings, and the lips curve forward over the teeth above a defined chin and jaw angle. The paint adds only brows, almond-shaped eyes and lip colour. Hair is left off the base figure.
  • An athletic V-taper: broad shoulders and chest, a narrow waist, a strong neck and long tapering limbs.
  • Solid colours with no painted detail, apart from thin lines for the eyes, brows and mouth.
  • Bodies are procedural too. The same seed sets head width, head size, neck length, shoulder width, torso length, chest or bust, waist, hips, glutes, leg length, knee height, arm length and overall build and muscle tone (biceps, triceps, deltoids, forearms, quads, hamstrings, calves, traps and a hint of abs, shaped into denser limb meshes). Clothing would follow automatically because it is built from the same shapes.
  • Faces are procedural. A seed sets about 30 values: head width, depth and length, jaw width, chin size and projection, brow ridge, cheekbones, eye height, spacing, depth and size, nose length, width, tip and hump, lip fullness, mouth width, forehead slope and ears. The same seed also picks the skin tone, eye colour, brow colour and brow shape. Hair is left off the base figure: length and style will be a procedural layer, like clothing. Each recruit could carry just one number.
  • The same skeleton as the game's animation rig, so the walk cycle and every existing pose apply unchanged.
