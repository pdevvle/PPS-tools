Base figure study

A plain starting point for the characters in a faceted low-poly style. The forms are built from flat planes, with an angular head, a separate cap of hair, an athletic build and solid colours. There are no textures except a few lines for the face. Clothing, hair styles and gear would layer on top later.

What defines the look

  • Flat shading on purpose. Each form has 8 to 12 sides, so the light breaks into clear planes across the chest, jaw, shoulders and limbs.
  • A narrow, angular head: a squared jaw, a defined chin, a flat face, a wedge nose and a high forehead, under a separate faceted cap of hair.
  • An athletic V-taper: broad shoulders and chest, a narrow waist, a strong neck and long tapering limbs.
  • Solid colours with no painted detail, apart from thin lines for the eyes, brows and mouth.
  • The same skeleton as the game's animation rig, so the walk cycle and every existing pose apply unchanged.
