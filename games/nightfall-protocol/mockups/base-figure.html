Character style · base figure

Base figure study

A plain starting point for the characters: two unclothed base bodies with realistic, slightly lanky proportions and a relaxed stance. Anatomy is painted into low-resolution skin textures rather than modelled, the way Project Zomboid does it. Clothing, hair and gear would layer on top of these later.

What makes it read as grounded, not goofy

  • The head is about a sixth of body height and longer than it is wide. The neck is short.
  • Shoulders are narrow: about twice the head's width. The limbs are thin and taper, and the legs make up more than half the height.
  • Arms hang straight and a little away from the body, with the fingertips below the crotch.
  • No outlines, no bright colour and no oversized features. Shading is painted in at low resolution, so up close it looks like a texture, not a cartoon.
  • The skeleton matches the game's animation rig, so the walk cycle and every existing pose would apply unchanged.
