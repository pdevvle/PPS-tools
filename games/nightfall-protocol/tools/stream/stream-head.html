New River, walking between sectors

A 3 km block of real New River, Arizona, baked offline into 25 sectors of 600 m that share their edges exactly. The page keeps the 3 by 3 sectors around your squad loaded: the one they stand in at full detail with ink outlines, the eight around it lighter. Walk toward an edge and the next row loads while the far one is dropped. Click the ground to move; paths cross sector edges.

How it streams

  • The whole block is shaped once, offline: washes, roads, bridges, pads, plants and the movement grid. Each sector file then carries its own slice, so nothing is computed when it loads except meshes.
  • Sector files are fetched on demand and built a step at a time between frames, so loading spreads over several frames instead of freezing one.
  • The centre sector gets ink outlines and creases; the ring around it doesn't. When the squad crosses an edge, outlines move with them.
  • Sectors two away from the squad are dropped and their memory released.
