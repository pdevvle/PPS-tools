Nightfall · strategic layer prototype

I-17 corridor, Arizona

The pilot region as a stylised 3D landscape, built from real OpenStreetMap data and elevation and cut into 600 m sectors. Knowledge of each sector fades from current to scouted to rumoured to unknown. Pick a squad, click a sector and send them: they follow real roads, the clock runs, and they reveal what they pass. Mind the heat and the water, search the places you find and bring the haul home, and watch for raiders. The campaign saves in this browser.
