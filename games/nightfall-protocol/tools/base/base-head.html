Log

    Found it anywhereA base is a 140 m square on the 2 m grid. Buildings standing on it are claimed. The biggest house is home and holds the water barrels, the next is the store and the next the workshop. Small outbuildings can be taken apart. A base with a house has an old well whose pump died with the grid.
    Built things change the gridWalls, tanks, the bunkhouse and watch posts write wall or building and their cover, as the bake does. Barricades, kitchens and workbenches give half cover. Gates shut at night and always let the base's own people through.
    Shared rulesSurvivors are People records: their bodies, moods and choice of work come from the people simulation. Building offers tasks in the shared shape and sets the day: outdoor work 06–10 and 18–22, half speed in the heat, sleep 22–06 with two on watch. The stores stand in until settlement is wired.
