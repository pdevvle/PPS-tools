Microsoft Advertising tip

Turn on UTM auto-tagging in your Microsoft Advertising account-level options. Microsoft then tags every click for you (search clicks arrive as bing / cpc), so there's nothing to add to each ad.

Naming tips

  • Use lowercase and dashes: fall-business-cards, not Fall Business Cards.
  • Keep source and medium consistent. Analytics treats Facebook and facebook as two sources.
  • Use content to tell apart creatives in the same campaign.
