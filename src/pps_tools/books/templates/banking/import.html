How it works

  1. Transactions land in For review — nothing hits your books until you add them.
  2. Each one gets a suggestion: a match to something you already entered (an invoice payment, a check), one of your bank rules, or the category you used last time for that payee.
  3. Uploading the same file twice is safe: transactions already imported are skipped.
  4. Payouts from Stripe arrive as transfers from your Stripe balance and match automatically.
