Year-end checklist

  1. Reconcile every bank and credit card account through year end.
  2. Review A/R aging and write off what won't be collected.
  3. Check unpaid bills and uncleared transactions.
  4. Count stock with the physical inventory worksheet and adjust quantities.
  5. Record depreciation and other adjusting journal entries.
  6. Review the 1099 summary before January 31.
  7. Look over the P&L and balance sheet, then set the closing date.

Net income rolls into Retained Earnings automatically at the start of each fiscal year; there are no closing entries to make. Exceptions to closing date lists any change made to a closed period afterwards.
