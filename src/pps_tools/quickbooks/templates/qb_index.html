How it works

  1. Connect your QuickBooks Online company.
  2. We pull transactions posted to Uncategorized Expense / Ask My Accountant (or any transactions in a date range if you prefer).
  3. Claude suggests a category from your chart of accounts with a confidence score and reasoning.
  4. You approve, edit, or skip each suggestion — approved changes are pushed back to QBO.
